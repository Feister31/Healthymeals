# NutriPlan – Smart Meal Planning Platform

## 🎯 Project Vision

NutriPlan is an intelligent meal planning and nutrition tracking application designed to help users make healthy eating effortless, affordable, and personalized. The platform bridges the gap between nutrition goals and real-world grocery shopping, enabling users to plan their week, track their protein intake, manage their grocery budget, and build a sustainable meal routine.

### Core Philosophy

**From Chaos to Clarity**: Meal planning shouldn't be overwhelming. NutriPlan transforms the complex process of weekly meal planning into a streamlined, visual, and intuitive experience.

**Data-Driven Nutrition**: By integrating with Airtable, users maintain complete control over their recipe database while leveraging smart algorithms that suggest optimal meal combinations based on their protein goals.

**Budget-Conscious Health**: Healthy eating doesn't have to be expensive. NutriPlan's price-aware features help users filter recipes by cost, estimate grocery bills, and make informed decisions about their food budget.
